## Purpose

This registry classifies unresolved wiki-link targets without deleting links or creating empty placeholder pages.

**Snapshot date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>

**Scope:** outgoing `[[wikilinks]]` in `02-Wiki/*.md`

**Method:** target existence checked against Markdown filenames across the vault
